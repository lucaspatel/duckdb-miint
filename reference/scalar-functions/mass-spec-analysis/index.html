Mass spec analysis

Computational helpers for mass-spectrometry analysis. Currently in this category:

  • formula — chemical-formula → monoisotopic mass (Da). Useful for building target lists, checking adduct identities, and validating precursor m/z assignments.

The mzML/mzXML readers (read_mzml, read_mzml_chromatograms, read_mzxml) live under mzml-io; the MassQL transpiler (massql) and per-spectrum operators are also separate categories.

formulaCompute the monoisotopic mass (Daltons) of a chemical formula like 'H2O', 'C6H12O6', or 'Fe'.—
