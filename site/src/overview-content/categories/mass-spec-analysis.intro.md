Computational helpers for mass-spectrometry analysis. Currently in this
category:

- **`formula`** — chemical-formula → monoisotopic mass (Da). Useful for
  building target lists, checking adduct identities, and validating
  precursor m/z assignments.

The mzML/mzXML readers (`read_mzml`, `read_mzml_chromatograms`,
`read_mzxml`) live under [mzml-io](../mzml-io/); the MassQL transpiler
(`massql`) and per-spectrum operators are also separate categories.
