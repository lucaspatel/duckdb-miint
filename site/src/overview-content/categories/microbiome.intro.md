Microbiome-specific table functions and analyses. Covers reading
feature/sample matrices, classifying sequences, clustering, and
chimera detection.

Currently in this category: `read_biom` (BIOM v2.1 sparse-COO reader).
The `woltka_ogu` OGU feature-table generator, the `cluster_sequences` /
`search_sequences` vsearch wrappers, and the `uchime_*` chimera
detectors will land here as their registrations get converted.

For per-feature taxonomic classification see
[**rype**](../rype/) instead.
